About

Currently, I am the Head of Computer Vision/AI at BotLab Dynamics / Vayudh, where I lead the development of next-generation intelligence for drones. My team's mission is to engineer robust, autonomous systems capable of thriving in highly challenging, real-world scenarios.

Prior ot this, I spent 5+ years as a Senior Research Scientist and Principal Investigator at TCS Research, IIT-Delhi R&I Park. Leading a dedicated research team, I drove initiatives at the intersection of Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Graphics. I oversaw the full lifecycle of innovation, from formulating research strategies and mentoring scientists and researchers to piloting advanced prototyps with enterprise clients.

Prior to joining TCS Research, I worked at NEC Laboratories America, with Prof. Manmohan Chandraker, Dr. Quoc-Huy Tran, and Dr. Pan Ji, where I developed world's first self-improving framework for SLAM. I received my PhD from IIIT-Delhi, with the Doctoral Dissertation Award. I was advised by Dr. Saket Anand and funded by prestegious Visvesvarya PhD fellowship. I am the recipient of Chancellor’s Gold Medal for academic excellence in B.Tech (Computer Science and Engineering) 2008-2012.
